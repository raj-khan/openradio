# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

OpenRadio is a small, friendly project about listening to the world. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background, country, language, faith, ability or experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept that people have different tastes, contexts and constraints.
- Respect that stations belong to their broadcasters and communities.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks, or dismissing people based on where they are from or what they listen to.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it privately by opening a GitHub security advisory or contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow this code, and may block repeat offenders. Decisions aim to keep this a calm and welcoming place.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
